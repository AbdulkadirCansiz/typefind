Type it. Find it.

TypeFind shows what you are looking for while you are still typing: files on your PC, and tabs, bookmarks and history in your browser.

TypeFind for Windows

Instant file and folder search. Press a shortcut, type part of a name, open the result. An index of file names is kept ready in the background, so results appear in milliseconds.

Coming soon to Microsoft Store

TypeFind for Chrome and Edge

One search box for open tabs, bookmarks, history and recently closed tabs. Jump to a tab or reopen a page without leaving the keyboard.

Coming soon to Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons

What makes it different

  • Results as you type. No waiting, no "searching…" spinner.
  • Forgiving. Upper or lower case, accents and small typos do not matter: resume finds Résumé, quartely finds Quarterly.
  • Any word order. Type the words you remember, in any order.
  • Keyboard first. Open, move, choose and close without touching the mouse.
  • Private. Everything happens on your device. TypeFind does not send your data anywhere. See the privacy policy.
